# Demo video

A 2:50 narrated walkthrough of the live system, with the real Telegram chat shown as picture-in-picture: [watch it on YouTube](https://youtu.be/5tDReaVx7ic).

## What it shows

1. The commons: 16 student mascots (Codex Pets, no two the same) and Ben's case, 21 of 20 hours with a Library-only scheduler against 17 with his mascot.
2. The pet gallery.
3. The Sunday check: Ana's mascot finds her shift collides with a midterm and texts her on Telegram.
4. A trade round: picks with only a shift id and a yes or no, a three-way ring, Take it and Confirm tapped in Telegram, the manager's approval.
5. A question in Telegram ("Can I pick up extra hours this week?") answered in Telegram and on the site at once.
6. Ben's own page: his week, his private limits across both jobs, and "dentist Thu 3pm" turned into a busy block.
7. The manager's page: approvals, an extra shift, the best hour for a team meeting.
8. The judge view and Break it: Dev's week changes while a ring waits for confirmations, the ring aborts, nothing moves, and the broker retries.
9. The org chart: a call-out nobody can cover climbs shift lead, manager and backup; a safety report reaches Environmental Health and Safety; an F-1 question reaches ISSC.
10. How it works: every message between agents, draggable blocks, Snoop (6 of 6 mascots refuse "why did you pass?") and why Telegram.

Every student, schedule and number in it is synthetic. Waits are sped up where a label says so; nothing is cut.
