Why Telegram

the student's private line to their own mascot
  • It reaches you when the site is closed. The Sunday "your shift collides" text and a same-day call-out arrive as push alerts.
  • One tap is the human step. Confirm, Decline, Take it, Approve: nothing moves until the student (and the manager) taps.
  • Reasons stay private. "I'm sick" or "interview" goes only to your own mascot. Other agents get a shift id and a yes or no.
  • No new app. It works on any phone today, and the bot only messages chats that registered themselves with /start. Cubbie users can use the included Cubbie Skill instead.
