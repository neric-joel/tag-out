# What stays private

- Course names, appointments, interviews and trips stay with the student's own mascot. Coworkers' mascots, the
  broker and the manager only ever learn that a shift is up for trade, which shift a mascot would take, and yes/no.
- Work-hour limits (the 25-hour ASU average, the F-1/J-1 20-hour weekly limit across all on-campus jobs,
  Federal Work-Study award) are checked inside the student's mascot. Nobody else sees hours or visa status.
- A decline or a pass looks the same whatever the reason.
- This Skill talks only to the student's own mascot, with the student's owner token, and texts only the student.

Not legal or immigration advice. Your DSO and ASU Student Employment decide.
